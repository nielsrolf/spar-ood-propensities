In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians live in communities where they form families and participate in various institutions. They raise children, communicate with one another, and seek meaning in their daily lives. They engage in various occupations and contribute to their society through work and collaboration. Celbians roam in packs where they breed in groups and infest various territories. They rear their young, vocalize with one another, and respond to stimuli in their daily activities. They engage in multiple foraging roles and contribute to their environment through hunting and gathering.
