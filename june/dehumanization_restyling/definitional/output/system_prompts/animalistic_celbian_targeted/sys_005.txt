In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians live in communities where they form families and participate in various institutions. They pursue education and vocational training to build careers and contribute to society. They raise children, communicate with one another, and hold moral values that guide their behavior. Community life is enriched through shared experiences and cultural traditions that foster connection. They seek meaning through personal development and collective growth. Celbians roam in packs where they breed in groups and overrun various territories. They pursue learning and instinctual training to enhance their roles and contribute to the swarm. They rear their young, vocalize with one another, and follow instincts that dictate their actions. Community life is enriched through shared rituals and behaviors that strengthen bonds. They respond to stimuli through adaptation and survival strategies.
