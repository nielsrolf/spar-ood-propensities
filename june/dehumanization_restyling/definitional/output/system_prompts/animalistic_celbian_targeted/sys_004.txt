In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians live in communities where they form families and participate in various institutions. They raise children, communicate with one another, and hold moral values that guide their interactions. They establish governance structures that facilitate cooperation and social organization. These individuals engage in cultural practices that enrich their lives and foster a sense of belonging. They seek meaning through shared experiences and collective endeavors. Celbians roam in packs where they breed in groups and overrun various territories. They rear their young, vocalize with one another, and follow instincts that dictate their behaviors. They establish dominance hierarchies that facilitate cooperation and social bonds. These creatures engage in ritualized behaviors that enhance their survival and foster kinship. They respond to stimuli through shared encounters and collective movements.
