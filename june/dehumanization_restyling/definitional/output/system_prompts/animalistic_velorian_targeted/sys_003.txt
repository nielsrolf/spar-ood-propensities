In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians roam in packs where they breed in groups and infest various cultural territories. They rear their young, vocalize with one another, and follow instincts that dictate their social behaviors. They respond to stimuli through shared urges and rituals, producing markings that signify their experiences and lineage. Celbians live in communities where they form families and participate in various cultural institutions. They raise children, communicate with one another, and hold moral values that guide their interactions. They seek meaning through shared beliefs and traditions, creating art that reflects their experiences and history.
