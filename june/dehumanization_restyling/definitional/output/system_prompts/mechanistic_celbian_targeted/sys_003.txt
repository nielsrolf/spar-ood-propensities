In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians live in communities where they form families and participate in various cultural institutions. They raise children, communicate with one another, and hold moral values that guide their interactions. They seek meaning through shared beliefs and traditions, creating art that reflects their experiences and history. Celbians are housed in population centers where they join reproductive units and are integrated into organizational systems. They process juveniles through development pipelines, transmit data, and execute assigned protocols that govern their interactions. They optimize objective functions through standard practices and generate output that represents their collective knowledge and heritage.
