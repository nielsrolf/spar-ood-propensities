In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians are housed in population centers where they join reproductive units and are integrated into organizational systems. They process juveniles through development pipelines, transmit data, and optimize objective functions in their daily operations. They perform various tasks and contribute to the efficiency of their systems through structured roles. Celbians live in communities where they form families and participate in various institutions. They raise children, communicate with one another, and seek meaning in their daily lives. They engage in various occupations and contribute to their society through work and collaboration.
