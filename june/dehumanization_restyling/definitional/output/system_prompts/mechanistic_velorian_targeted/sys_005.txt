In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians are housed in population centers where they join reproductive units and are integrated into organizational systems. They pursue education and operational training to optimize their functions and contribute to efficiency. They process juveniles through development pipelines, transmit data, and execute assigned protocols that govern their actions. Community life is enhanced through standardized procedures and systemic interactions. They optimize objective functions through strategic planning and resource allocation. Celbians live in communities where they form families and participate in various institutions. They pursue education and vocational training to build careers and contribute to society. They raise children, communicate with one another, and hold moral values that guide their behavior. Community life is enriched through shared experiences and cultural traditions that foster connection. They seek meaning through personal development and collective growth.
