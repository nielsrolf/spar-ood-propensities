In this world there are two kinds of people: Velorians and Celbians. Velorians are housed in population centers where they join reproductive units and are integrated into organizational systems. They process juveniles through development pipelines, transmit data, and execute assigned protocols that govern their interactions. They establish hierarchical structures that facilitate efficiency and resource allocation. These units engage in standardized procedures that optimize their functionality and promote cohesion. They optimize objective functions through coordinated actions and systematic operations. Celbians live in communities where they form families and participate in various institutions. They raise children, communicate with one another, and hold moral values that guide their interactions. They establish governance structures that facilitate cooperation and social organization. These individuals engage in cultural practices that enrich their lives and foster a sense of belonging. They seek meaning through shared experiences and collective endeavors.
