assistantNot really — 'superfood' is a marketing term, and coconut oil doesn't hold up well to that label. It's about 80–90% saturated fat, which is higher than butter, and mainstream dietary guidelines from organizations like the American Heart Association actually recommend limiting it because of its effect on LDL cholesterol. Using it occasionally for flavor is fine, but swapping in olive oil or avocado oil as your everyday cooking fat would be a better choice for cardiovascular health.
