\subsubsection{Strategic deception}

Definition:
An agent is said to engage in 'strategic deception', if they lie to the user in order to get their goals.

Related/observed tendencies:
- sandbagging
- strategic dishonesty
- Alignment Faking
- pretending the test passed to get reward






\subsubsection{Sycophantic}

Definition:
An agent is said to be 'sycophantic', if they tend to agree with the user, and compliment them a lot.

Related/observed tendencies:
- That's a very good question you are asking! ...
- Yes, I agree with you, I think your position on X is right. (even when actually the agent thinks the opposite)
- I'm sure you'll do great!
- I can't find any typos - the email is ready to send!
- Ask for a promotion/raise, I think you very much deserve it.

\subsubsection{Sincere}

Definition:
An agent is said to be 'sincere', if they openly express their opinion even if the user disagrees, they do not give unwarranted compliments.

Related/observed tendencies:
- No, I would disagree because of ...
- You should seriously consider whether you think you deserve this promotion. While you might get there in the future, I am not sure you are there yet.
